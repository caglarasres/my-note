Privacy policy

Effective 10 October 2026

In short. My Note has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your notebooks stay on your iPad. We collect nothing. The one purchase is handled by Apple.

Who we are

My Note ("the app") is made by Caglar Kurt, an independent developer. For anything about this policy, write to kurtcaglar777@gmail.com.

What the app keeps on your device

Your notebooks, handwriting, folders, the files you import and your settings are saved on your iPad only. The app does not send them anywhere and we never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them. If you have device backups switched on, Apple includes the app's data in your own backup, under Apple's terms.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not show ads and the app contains no advertising or analytics software.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not ask for your location, contacts, camera or microphone.
  • We do not sell or share personal information, because we have none.

Files you import

When you choose a PDF, document or picture to write on, the app makes its own copy inside its storage on your iPad. The original is not changed and the copy is not uploaded.

In-app purchase

My Note Pro can be bought once through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your card or payment details, your name or your Apple Account. Apple's handling of that information is described in Apple's privacy policy.

When you contact us

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to tell us. We use it only to answer you and do not add you to any list.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, children included. In-app purchases can be switched off on a child's device with Screen Time.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical details of visits, such as IP addresses, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. The site itself sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
