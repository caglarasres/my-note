Support

Something not working, or a question about My Note? Write to kurtcaglar777@gmail.com and you will get an answer from the person who makes it.

It helps to say which iPad you have, which version of iPadOS it runs, and what you were doing when the problem appeared.

Where are my notes kept?

On your iPad, inside the app. There is no account and nothing is sent to us. They are included in your iPad's own iCloud or computer backup if you have backups switched on.

How do I write on a PDF or on lecture slides?

In the library, tap the plus button and choose From a File, or open the file in another app, tap Share and choose My Note. PDFs, PowerPoint, Keynote, Word, Pages, spreadsheets and pictures become a notebook you can write on.

How do I get my notes out?

Open the notebook, tap the ⋯ button and choose to export it as a PDF. Your handwriting is included on top of the original pages.

My finger does not draw.

By default only Apple Pencil writes, so that your hand can rest on the screen. To write with a finger, open the ⋯ menu in a notebook and switch on Draw with Finger.

How do I straighten a shape?

Draw a line, box, circle, triangle or arrow with any pen and keep the Pencil still at the end for a moment. It becomes the true shape, and you can keep dragging to resize it.

I deleted a notebook by mistake.

In the library, tap ⋯ and choose Recently Deleted. Deleted notebooks are kept there for 30 days and can be restored with everything in them.

What does My Note Pro unlock, and how do I restore it?

The free version lets you create three notebooks. My Note Pro is one purchase, not a subscription, and removes that limit for good. Notebooks you already have are never locked. If you bought Pro before, open My Note Pro from the library's ⋯ menu and tap Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
